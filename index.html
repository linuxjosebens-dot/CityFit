How are parking sites recommended?

Results are mapped OpenStreetMap surface parking lots inside Irvine, filtered by distance from your selected address. Private lots, lots that explicitly prohibit trucks, and parking garages are excluded. Missing truck access or capacity stays unknown. Category labels describe the closest mapped venue within 0.35 miles; they do not establish ownership.

Past evidence ranks your recorded orders per hour for the selected menu at the exact lot, then published past food-truck events, historical City road counts, and distance. Venue event evidence does not identify the lot used by the trucks. Parking records cover selected Irvine areas and are incomplete. Pins indicate mapped lot centers; lot outlines and nearby measured roads come from source geometry. Confirm truck fit and host approval for the precise service bay.
