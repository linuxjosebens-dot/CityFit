How CityFit works

CityFit helps food-truck owners find parking options in Irvine and plan the cost of a shift.

Find a place
Plan a shift
Get ready

1. Find parking

Enter an address or allow location access. The website checks its saved parking records, filters them by distance and shows up to ten matching lots on the map.

Lots can be compared using distance, past shifts and traffic measurements. The records cover Irvine only. Past traffic does not tell us how many customers are there right now.

2. Estimate your earnings

Choose a lot and enter your expected orders, average sale and costs. The planner updates the estimate as you type.

Estimated earnings = sales − costs. It also shows how many orders you need to cover your costs. This is an estimate, not guaranteed profit.

3. Save and prepare

Save favorite lots, record past shifts and use the permit checklist. Download a shift plan as a PDF. The account area lets you sign in, choose an avatar and switch between light and dark mode.

Plans and saved records stay in that browser; signing in does not automatically copy them to another device. Confirm parking and vending permission before serving.
